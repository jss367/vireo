What this experiment does—and what it cannot establish

All seven treatments were rendered at native resolution with the same Vireo tone and color settings, without sharpening, clarity, or texture. Native images are lossless 8-bit sRGB PNGs made after floating-point rendering. Overview JPEGs are navigation aids, not sharpness evidence.

The neural model jointly denoises and demosaics. Its camera RGB is converted using a checked, per-photo calibration through Vireo's existing RAW decoder. This is an offline Python/ONNX CPU experiment inspired by Lightcraft's processing; it is not a benchmark of Lightcraft's Rust/GPU runtime. Strength numbers are not equivalent between methods.

This is a development set of user-flagged D850 photographs, not a held-out efficacy test. The selected Z8 High Efficiency* originals could not be decoded. There are no clean reference captures here, so a pleasant result does not prove that reconstructed markings are correct. Subject crops are manually selected viewing aids; the corner crop may contain texture rather than clean background.
